## Performance Checklist

### 1. Handle Housekeeping Requests
- [ ] Requests are acknowledged politely and promptly
- [ ] Guest request details are confirmed and recorded accurately
- [ ] Timelines are agreed with the guest
- [ ] Items are located, delivered, or picked up within the agreed time
- [ ] Appropriate follow-up is reported to the relevant person
- [ ] Guest is informed of progress when delays occur

### 2. Advise Guests on Equipment
- [ ] Equipment use is explained clearly and courteously
- [ ] Guest instructions are simple and relevant
- [ ] Malfunctions are reported promptly
- [ ] Alternative arrangements are offered where possible
- [ ] Collection time is agreed where appropriate

### 3. Deal with Guest Complaints
- [ ] Guest dissatisfaction is acknowledged immediately
- [ ] Complaint is identified clearly
- [ ] Guest is reassured that action will be taken
- [ ] Complaint is responded to calmly and politely
- [ ] Issues within authority are resolved promptly
- [ ] Issues beyond authority are escalated appropriately
- [ ] Complaint is recorded accurately and reported according to procedure

### 4. Deal with Guest Incidents
- [ ] Incident is identified and action begins without delay
- [ ] Guest is treated respectfully at all times
- [ ] Appropriate action is taken according to procedure
- [ ] Incident is reported to the correct person
- [ ] Company procedures are followed

### 5. Handle Room Change
- [ ] Room change instructions are carried out accurately
- [ ] Guest is informed clearly and politely
- [ ] Minimum inconvenience is achieved
- [ ] Guest belongings are transferred without damage
- [ ] Unexpected issues are reported and handled according to procedure
- [ ] Guest satisfaction is maintained as much as possible
