# Housekeeping Level 2 Training Program
## THHHOK0971B: Provide Housekeeping Services to Guests

**Duration:** 3 hours  
**Credit Value:** 3  
**Target Group:** Housekeeping Level 2 trainees  
**Competency Field:** Housekeeping Operations  
**Standard:** THHHOK0971B, NCTVET THH, Version B

---

## Training Package Overview
This interactive package is designed to help trainees meet the requirements of THHHOK0971B: Provide Housekeeping Services to Guests. It covers:

- Handling guest requests
- Advising guests on room and housekeeping equipment
- Managing complaints and incidents
- Executing room changes with professionalism and care
- Maintaining service standards, guest satisfaction, and safety

---

## Learning Outcomes
By the end of the training, trainees should be able to:

- Handle guest requests in line with hotel customer service standards and security procedures
- Confirm and record details of requests accurately
- Communicate timelines clearly and professionally
- Deliver and retrieve items within agreed times
- Setup equipment appropriately for guests
- Advise guests on equipment use and report faults promptly
- Respond to complaints and incidents calmly and correctly
- Execute room changes with minimum inconvenience to guests
- Protect guest property and ensure smooth service delivery

## Suggested Delivery Sequence
1. Introduction and learning objectives
2. Guest request handling and service standards
3. Equipment guidance and guest support
4. Complaint management and incident response
5. Room change scenario and practical exercise
6. Quiz and reflection

---

## Assessment Summary
- Observation during role-play and scenario practice
- Oral questioning during discussion
- Written quiz at the end of the session
- Performance checklist for room change handling and guest service

---

## Trainer Notes
Use a practical, hotel-based approach. Trainees should practice speaking politely, taking notes clearly, and following property procedures. Emphasize problem-solving, professionalism, and guest safety.
