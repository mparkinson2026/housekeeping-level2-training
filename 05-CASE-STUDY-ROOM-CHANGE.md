# Case Study / Scenario: Room Change
## THHHOK0971B: Provide Housekeeping Services to Guests

**Scenario Title:** Room Change Due to Noise Disturbance and Bathroom Issue  
**Context:** A guest is checked into the hotel and has been experiencing disturbance from a neighboring room. The guest is also unhappy because the bathroom fan has not been working properly during the previous evening. The guest requests a room change and says they are very upset and tired.

---

## Background
Room 412 is occupied by Mr. Daniel James, a business guest. He has been staying for two nights and reports that:
- noise from the neighboring room is preventing rest
- the bathroom fan is not functioning
- the guest has had a poor sleep and is frustrated
- he wants a quieter room and minimal inconvenience

The duty supervisor tells the housekeeping team to arrange a room move to Room 305, which is a quiet room on a different corridor. The room is available and ready. The guest has a work bag, clothing, toiletries, laptop, and a small suitcase.

---

## Scenario Task
As a housekeeping trainee, explain how you would handle the situation according to THHHOK0971B.

---

## Case Study Questions

### 1. What should you do first when the guest requests a room change?
**Example answer:**
- Listen calmly and acknowledge the concern
- Ask for the specific reason and confirm the request
- Note the guest's room number, preferred change, and issues reported
- Inform the supervisor and confirm the new room is available

---

### 2. How would you communicate with the guest during the move?
**Example answer:**
- Speak politely and respectfully
- Apologize for the inconvenience
- Explain the process briefly and reassure the guest that the move will be arranged with care
- Keep them informed of timing and support

---

### 3. What steps should be taken before moving the guest’s belongings?
**Example answer:**
- Check the new room and ensure it is ready
- Confirm the move time with the guest
- Prepare a trolley or cart if required
- Ensure luggage and personal items are handled carefully
- Check that the new room is clean and suitable

---

### 4. How should guest property be protected during the room transfer?
**Example answer:**
- Handle luggage carefully
- Avoid stacking items in a way that can damage them
- Use proper moving method and equipment
- Keep electronic items secure
- Check if any item is fragile or needs special handling

---

### 5. What if the guest is still dissatisfied after the move?
**Example answer:**
- Listen to the concern without interruption
- Acknowledge the dissatisfaction sincerely
- Refer the issue to the supervisor or manager if required
- Offer additional support, such as a quieter room, fan check, or service recovery action
- Record the complaint according to property procedures

---

## Role-Play Script

### Guest Line
“I'm tired and I cannot sleep because of the noise next door. Also, the bathroom fan is not working. I need a quieter room and I want this handled without further disruption.”

### Staff Response Example
“I’m very sorry for the inconvenience. I understand that this has affected your rest. I will arrange a room change as quickly as possible and I will take care of your belongings while moving them. I will confirm the details with the supervisor and let you know the timing.”

---

## Expected Practical Performance Criteria
The trainee should show that they can:
- [ ] Listen carefully and respond politely
- [ ] Confirm the room change request accurately
- [ ] Inform the appropriate person promptly
- [ ] Arrange the move with minimal inconvenience
- [ ] Handle guest belongings without damage
- [ ] Report any unexpected issue according to property procedures
- [ ] Maintain professionalism and guest satisfaction

---

## Trainer Debrief Questions
- Was the trainee empathetic and calm?
- Were instructions clear and accurate?
- Did the trainee protect guest property?
- Did they maintain hotel standards and confidentiality?
- Did they know when to escalate?

---

## Scenario Summary
This case highlights the importance of respectful communication, efficient room change coordination, and careful handling of guest belongings. The trainee must balance guest satisfaction with procedure, safety, and professionalism.
