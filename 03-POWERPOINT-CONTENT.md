## THHHOK0971B: Provide Housekeeping Services to Guests

---

## Slide 1: Title Slide
**THHHOK0971B: Provide Housekeeping Services to Guests**  
**Housekeeping Level 2 Training**  
**Unit: Housekeeping Operations**  
**Duration: 3 Hours**

---

## Slide 2: Unit Overview
- This unit covers general housekeeping services to guests
- Applies to housekeeping attendants, porters, and concierge staff
- Focus on service quality, professionalism, and guest satisfaction

---

## Slide 3: Learning Outcomes
By the end of the training, trainees will be able to:
- Handle guest requests professionally
- Advise on room and housekeeping equipment
- Respond to complaints and incidents
- Complete room changes with care and efficiency

---

## Slide 4: Competency Areas
1. Handle housekeeping requests
2. Advise guests on room and housekeeping equipment
3. Deal with guest complaints
4. Deal with guest related incidents
5. Handle room change

---

## Slide 5: Housekeeping Requests
Key points:
- Confirm guest request details
- Note request in a suitable format
- Apologize if service failure occurred
- Agree on timelines
- Deliver and retrieve items promptly
- Report progress to supervisor

---

## Slide 6: Request Handling Best Practice
- Listen carefully to the guest
- Ask clarifying questions
- Record details accurately
- Confirm timing and responsibility
- Update the guest on progress
- Escalate if unable to complete the request

---

## Slide 7: Security and Service Standards
- Follow property procedures at all times
- Respect guest privacy and confidentiality
- Maintain security awareness
- Never disclose room or guest information unnecessarily
- Handle lost items and deliveries carefully

---

## Slide 8: Equipment Advice to Guests
Examples of room equipment:
- Hair dryer
- Iron and ironing board
- Kettle and coffee maker
- Fan and air-conditioning controls
- Safe and general room comfort items

---

## Slide 9: Advising Guests Correctly
- Be courteous and clear
- Explain instructions simply
- Ensure safe usage
- Report defects immediately
- Offer alternatives if equipment fails
- Agree collection time if equipment is borrowed

---

## Slide 10: Report Malfunctions Promptly
If equipment is faulty:
- Do not ignore it
- Report to the appropriate person
- Record details
- Offer temporary alternative if available
- Inform the guest of next action

---

## Slide 11: Guest Complaints
A guest complaint can be about:
- room cleanliness
- missing items
- late service
- equipment failure
- noise or room discomfort
- poor communication or delay

---

## Slide 12: Complaint Handling Steps
1. Acknowledge immediately
2. Listen attentively
3. Identify the complaint
4. Apologize appropriately
5. Act within authority
6. Escalate if necessary
7. Record and report accurately

---

## Slide 13: Complaint Example
A guest says: “I asked for extra towels two hours ago and no one has brought them.”  
Response should include:
- immediate apology
- acknowledgement of delay
- confirm request details
- arrange delivery and follow-up
- update supervisor if needed

---

## Slide 14: Guest Incidents
Examples of incidents:
- spill in corridor
- guest reports damaged property
- suspicious behavior
- guest injury or distress
- equipment-related safety concern

---

## Slide 15: Incident Response Principles
- Identify issue quickly
- Follow property procedures
- Stay calm and polite
- Reassure the guest
- Report immediately to the correct person
- Keep accurate records

---

## Slide 16: Room Changes
Room change is a special service requiring care and professionalism.

Key actions:
- Follow room change instructions accurately
- Inform the guest clearly and politely
- Minimize inconvenience and disruption
- Transfer guest effects without damage
- Confirm the room is suitable and comfortable

---

## Slide 17: Room Change Procedure
1. Receive and confirm room change instructions
2. Check availability of new room
3. Explain the move to the guest respectfully
4. Arrange timing with minimal disruption
5. Transfer belongings carefully
6. Report any unexpected issue
7. Ensure the guest is satisfied

---

## Slide 18: Guest Communication During Room Change
Say examples:
- “We understand this is inconvenient, and we will do our best to minimize disruption.”
- “I will arrange the move as quickly as possible and ensure your belongings are handled carefully.”
- “We will assist with the transfer and make sure the room is ready for you.”

---

## Slide 19: Risk and Safety Points
- Protect guest belongings
- Avoid damage to luggage, electronics, or fixtures
- Keep guest property secure during transfer
- Report any breakages or unusual situations immediately
- Maintain privacy and respect during the move

---

## Slide 20: Key Skills for Successful Service
- Listening
- Empathy
- Time management
- Accurate documentation
- Professional communication
- Teamwork and escalation

---

## Slide 21: Group Activity
Role-play scenarios:
- Delayed room service request
- Guest complaint about dirty towels
- Equipment malfunction in a room
- Room change due to noise disturbance

Discuss:
- What should be done first?
- Who should be informed?
- How should the guest be spoken to?

---

## Slide 22: Quick Recap
A good housekeeping staff member must:
- respond promptly
- communicate politely
- act within procedures
- document clearly
- protect guest property and comfort
- maintain service standards at all times

---

At the end of the lesson, trainees will complete:

- practical scenario responses
