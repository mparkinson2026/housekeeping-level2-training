# End-of-Lesson Quiz with Answers
## THHHOK0971B: Provide Housekeeping Services to Guests

**Instructions:** Choose the best answer for each question.  
**Time:** 20 minutes

---

## Quiz Questions

### Question 1
When a guest requests extra towels, what is the first action a housekeeping staff member should take?
A. Ignore until the room is cleaned  
B. Confirm the request details and note them clearly  
C. Ask the guest to come back later  
D. Wait for the supervisor to act  

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 2
Which of the following best shows good guest service when a request cannot be completed immediately?
A. “There is nothing I can do.”  
B. “I am sorry for the delay. I will check the timing and update you.”  
C. “You should have asked earlier.”  
D. “I will do it when I get time.”  

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 3
If a guest reports a faulty kettle, what should the staff member do first?
A. Replace it without informing anyone  
B. Report the malfunction and inform the guest accordingly  
C. Leave it in the room and do nothing  
D. Blame the guest for using it incorrectly  

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 4
Which of the following is the correct response to a guest complaint?
A. Avoid the guest until the problem is solved  
B. Acknowledge the complaint immediately and listen carefully  
C. Tell the guest to submit a complaint form online  
D. Ask the guest to stop complaining  

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 5
What is the purpose of recording guest complaints and requests?
A. To fill time records only  
B. To provide accurate information to the appropriate person and maintain service standards  
C. To punish staff  
D. To avoid guest contact  

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 6
When handling a guest complaint that is outside the staff member’s authority, what should be done?
A. Try to hide the problem  
B. Refer it promptly to the appropriate person  
C. Tell the guest to wait outside  
D. Ignore it until the shift ends  

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 7
During a room change, which action is most important?
A. Move the guest quickly without speaking to them  
B. Minimize inconvenience and protect the guest’s belongings  
C. Change the room without informing the guest  
D. Move the items at any cost regardless of damage  

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 8
Which of the following is a good example of polite communication during a room move?
A. “You have to move because the manager says so.”  
B. “We understand this is inconvenient. We will help make the move as smooth as possible.”  
C. “This is your problem now.”  
D. “No one else is staying here.”  

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 9
A guest reports a suspicious incident in the corridor. What is the best immediate action?
A. Ignore it because it is not housekeeping-related  
B. Confirm the details and immediately report according to property procedures  
C. Speak loudly to scare the person away  
D. Call the guest by name in front of others  

**Answer:** B

---

### Question 10
What should be done when housekeeping equipment is malfunctioning?
A. Leave it until the next shift  
B. Use it anyway  
C. Report it promptly and arrange an alternative if possible  
D. Hide it from the guest  

**Answer:** C

---

## Short Answer Questions

### Question 11
List three important details a staff member should confirm when handling a guest request.

**Possible answer:**
- Guest name and room number
- Exact item or service requested
- Time or deadline required

---

### Question 12
State two ways to maintain a polite and professional attitude when dealing with complaints.

**Possible answer:**
- Listen without interrupting
- Speak calmly and respectfully

---

### Question 13
Why is it important to protect guest effects during a room change?

**Possible answer:**
To avoid damage, maintain guest trust, and meet property standards and guest satisfaction expectations.
