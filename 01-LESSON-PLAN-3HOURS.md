# 3-Hour Interactive Lesson Plan
## THHHOK0971B: Provide Housekeeping Services to Guests

**Course Title:** Housekeeping Services to Guests  
**Duration:** 3 hours  
**Target Learners:** Housekeeping Level 2 trainees  
**Method:** Interactive workshop with demonstrations, role-play, discussion, and scenario practice  
**Assessment:** Quiz + observation checklist

---

## Unit Purpose
This unit develops the knowledge, skills, and attitude required to provide a range of general housekeeping services to guests in commercial accommodation establishments.

---

## Learning Objectives
By the end of the session, trainees will be able to:

- Handle guest requests according to service standards and security procedures
- Record requests accurately and agree on timelines
- Advise guests on the safe and correct use of room and housekeeping equipment
- Report and resolve basic equipment or service issues
- Respond politely and promptly to guest complaints and incidents
- Handle room change instructions professionally and efficiently
- Protect guest property and minimize inconvenience during room moves

---

## Lesson Breakdown

### 1. Introduction and Orientation (10 minutes)
**Objectives:**
- Set the context of guest services in housekeeping
- Introduce competency standard and learning outcomes

**Activities:**
- Welcome and agenda overview
- Brief discussion: “What does a good housekeeping service look like?”
- Explain the importance of professionalism, safety, and guest satisfaction

**Trainer Notes:**
- Emphasize teamwork, efficiency, and respect for guest privacy
- Link to organizational standards and property procedures

---

### 2. Handling Housekeeping Requests (45 minutes)
**Time:** 45 minutes

**Content:**
- Guest request types (extra towels, linens, iron, kettle, toiletries, room setup, luggage assistance, wake-up call support)
- Confirming and noting details
- Customer service standards
- Security procedures
- Apology when service breakdown occurs
- Agreed timelines
- Delivery and pickup of items
- Reporting follow-up actions to supervisor
- Equipment setup and guest information

**Interactive Activities:**
- Request-taking exercise: trainees practice writing a request log entry
- Pair work: one trainee acts as guest, one as staff member
- Role-play: handling a delayed room service request

**Assessment Points:**
- Can the trainee confirm details clearly?
- Did the trainee show empathy and professionalism?
- Can the trainee communicate a realistic timeline?

---

### 3. Break (5 minutes)

---

### 4. Advising Guests on Room and Housekeeping Equipment (35 minutes)
**Time:** 35 minutes

**Content:**
- Correct use of room equipment (iron, kettle, coffee maker, fan, AC, hair dryer, safe, laundry bag)
- Advice standards: polite, clear, brief, practical
- Reporting equipment malfunctions
- Alternative arrangements for guest needs
- Agreeing collection time for borrowed equipment

**Interactive Activities:**
- Equipment demonstration and guest explanation drill
- Scenario: kettle not working; trainee must explain instructions and alternatives
- Discussion: what to do when a guest misuses equipment or damages it

**Assessment Points:**
- Did the trainee explain instructions clearly?
- Did the trainee maintain a courteous tone?
- Did they escalate malfunctions correctly?

---

### 5. Dealing with Guest Complaints (35 minutes)
**Time:** 35 minutes

**Content:**
- Immediate acknowledgement of dissatisfaction
- Calm and polite response
- Identifying complaint type
- Assuring guest of quick attention
- Resolving within authority
- Escalation to supervisor/appropriate person
- Recording complaint details accurately
- Reporting according to property procedures

**Interactive Activities:**
- Complaint role-play: noisy room, dirty towels, delayed cleaning, incorrect room setup
- Group discussion: what is within staff authority and what must be escalated?
- Complaint log exercise: write concise, factual entries

**Assessment Points:**
- Did trainee listen without interruption?
- Did they apologize appropriately?
- Did they follow escalation and documentation process?

---

### 6. Guest Incidents and Emergency Response (20 minutes)
**Time:** 20 minutes

**Content:**
- Identifying the nature of the incident
- Taking immediate action according to instructions
- Polite guest communication
- Reporting incidents promptly to management
- Following company procedures

**Examples:**
- Guest finds damaged property
- Guest reports suspicious activity
- Minor injury or spill in corridor
- Guest is upset and requires assistance

**Activity:**
- Quick case discussion and response planning in pairs

---

### 7. Room Change Process and Handling Guest Relocation (25 minutes)
**Time:** 25 minutes

**Content:**
- Carrying out room change instructions accurately
- Minimizing inconvenience to guest
- Maintaining guest satisfaction
- Handling unexpected situations
- Ensuring guest effects are transferred without damage
- Polite communication and reassurance

**Interactive Activity:**
- Case study: guest requests a room move due to noise and must be transferred to another room
- Team planning exercise: what steps must be taken?
- Demonstration of room change checklist

**Assessment Points:**
- Accurate action steps followed
- Guest communication maintained
- Property protection ensured

---

### 8. Review and Quiz (20 minutes)
**Time:** 20 minutes

**Activities:**
- Quick recap of key points
- Q&A discussion
- Written quiz
- Brief feedback

## Core Trainer Reminders
- The guest is always treated with respect and courtesy
- Follow property procedures and security rules
- Record all details clearly and promptly
- Escalate when not within authority
- Minimize disturbance during room movement
- Protect hotel property and guest belongings
