# Role-Play Scripts
## THHHOK0971B: Provide Housekeeping Services to Guests

---

## Script 1: Request for Extra Towels
**Guest:** “Hi, I need extra towels for the bathroom. We are staying for another two nights and I was told housekeeping could help.”  
**Staff:** “Certainly, thank you for letting me know. I will confirm the room number and quantity, and I will arrange the towels right away. I’ll have them delivered within the next 15 minutes.”  
**Guest:** “Thank you, that would be helpful.”  
**Staff:** “You’re welcome. I’ve noted your request and I’ll make sure it is delivered promptly.”

---

## Script 2: Guest Complaint About Delayed Service
**Guest:** “This is the second time I’ve had to ask for my room to be serviced. I am very upset.”  
**Staff:** “I’m very sorry for the delay and the inconvenience this has caused. I appreciate you telling me. I will check the service schedule and make sure your room is attended to today. I’ll also report this to the supervisor so it is followed up properly.”

---

## Script 3: Faulty Equipment
**Guest:** “The kettle in my room does not work, and I need it for coffee.”  
**Staff:** “I’m sorry about that. I will report the issue immediately and check whether we can provide a replacement or make alternative arrangements as quickly as possible.”

---

## Script 4: Noise Complaint and Room Change Request
**Guest:** “I cannot sleep because of the noise from the neighboring room. I need a quieter room.”  
**Staff:** “I understand and I’m sorry this is affecting your rest. I will check available room options and arrange a move with as little disruption as possible. I will make sure your belongings are handled carefully during the transfer.”

---

## Script 5: Complaint About Room Cleanliness
**Guest:** “The bathroom was not cleaned properly and the towels are damp.”  
**Staff:** “Thank you for informing me. I’m sorry this happened. I will report this immediately and arrange for the issue to be corrected without delay. I will make sure the room is attended to and the matter is documented correctly.”

---

## Facilitator Tips for Role-Play
- Use real hotel language and calm tone
- Encourage trainees to speak respectfully and clearly
- Require them to confirm details and follow-up actions
- Evaluate whether they escalate when needed
- Check whether they protect guest dignity and comfort

---

## Short Debrief Questions
- Was the response polite and respectful?
- Did the trainee confirm important detail?
- Did they apologize or acknowledge the issue?
- Did they offer a practical solution?
- Did they know when to escalate?
